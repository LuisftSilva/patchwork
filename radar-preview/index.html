Market & Companies11 Sep 2026 · DE / UK

RWB buys control of a European platform spanning Germany and the UK

Red White & Bloom closed a transaction giving it control of HEAL Europe Holdings. The company says the platform includes Grünhorn and other European assets, with more than 12 tonnes dispensed or distributed and about 290,000 prescriptions fulfilled in the 12 months ended July 2026.

Why it matters: consolidation is increasingly about owning patient access, pharmacy/distribution and logistics — not only cultivation.

Regulation & Policy11 Sep 2026 · DK

Denmark reports strong 2025 growth as the programme moves into its permanent phase

The Danish Medicines Agency reported that redeemed prescriptions rose 64% in 2025 and the number of citizens redeeming them rose 54% versus 2024. Three suspected adverse-reaction reports were received for programme cannabis products in 2025.

Why it matters: Denmark combines measurable demand growth with a permanent legal framework from 1 January 2026.
Official Danish Medicines Agency →
Market & Companies2 Sep 2026 · Global / Europe

Aurora tells shareholders to reject Curaleaf’s hostile bid

Aurora’s board rejected Curaleaf’s unsolicited offer, arguing that it undervalues the company and its international medical-cannabis platform. Aurora highlighted its international medical business as a central strategic asset.

Why it matters: EU-GMP capacity, distribution access and international medical revenue are becoming acquisition assets.

Market & CompaniesH1 2026 data · PT

Portugal reports 66.3 tonnes exported in six months

INFARMED’s August 2026 sector update reports 66,305 kg exported in H1 2026, 5,413 domestic prescriptions and 32 inspections. Germany, Spain and Denmark are identified among the main destinations. The figures cover plant, preparations and substances; they must not automatically be interpreted as retail flower.

Why it matters: Portugal’s role is increasingly industrial and export-led, while the form and final destination of cross-border product require separate analysis.
Official INFARMED cannabis hub →
Regulation & Policy28 Aug 2026 · DE

Germany introduces a reimbursement priority affecting cannabis-containing medicines

A Bundestag notice describes a reimbursement rule affecting standardized cannabis extracts and medicines containing dronabinol or nabilone, with implications for treatment sequencing and payer behaviour.

Why it matters: reimbursement rules can shift demand between finished medicines, extracts and compounded formulations even without changing the basic legality of medical cannabis.
Official Deutscher Bundestag →
Regulation & Policy11 Aug 2026 · ES

Spain’s standardized-cannabis preparation register is operational

AEMPS maintains the public register created under Royal Decree 903/2025 for standardized cannabis preparations that may be used in typified magistral formulas.

Why it matters: Spain has a defined pharmaceutical route for standardized preparations; this is not equivalent to an open German-style flower market.
Official AEMPS →
Quality / StandardsMay–Sep 2026 · EU

European Pharmacopoeia work programme includes a Cannabis flos revision

The EDQM work programme lists revision work for Cannabis flos, including ad hoc microbial-contamination limits for material administered by vaporisation. Quality teams should follow the final adopted text rather than secondary summaries.

Why it matters: pharmacopoeial revisions can alter specifications, testing and release expectations across European medicinal-cannabis supply chains.
Official EDQM work programme →
Market & Companies7 Sep 2026 · UK / DE

Wellford says its Canadian site renewed EU-GMP certification

Wellford reports that its Nova Scotia production facility renewed EU-GMP certification, preserving access to European medical-cannabis markets.

Why it matters: certification continuity is a strategic asset in transatlantic supply chains, but the statement remains company-supplied until independently checked in the relevant official register.
