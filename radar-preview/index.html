Current intelligence themes

These are defensible analytical directions supported by the current evidence base; not all are yet complete cross-country rankings.

Portugal is more visible as a production/export hub than as a domestic-demand market.

The current data is richer on production, trade, licences, GMP capacity and operators than on domestic patient demand. That makes Portugal especially useful for supply-chain and industrial-positioning analysis.

Portugal → Spain does not automatically mean retail flower.

Official Portuguese reporting aggregates plant, preparations and substances. Cross-border flows may represent raw material, intermediate processing, pharmaceutical manufacturing, research or re-export. The Radar preserves this distinction instead of labelling all exports as flower.

Denmark is a strong benchmark market.

Patients, prescriptions, volume, value, price and product attributes can be studied together, giving a clearer view of price × access × demand than in many larger but less transparent markets.

Strict derivation gates prevent misleading ratios.

If numerator and denominator do not describe the same product scope, period or patient population, the Radar blocks the derived metric rather than publishing a superficially plausible number.
