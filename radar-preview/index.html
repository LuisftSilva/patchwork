RWB buys control of a European platform spanning Germany and the UK

Red White & Bloom closed a transaction giving it control of HEAL Europe Holdings. The platform includes Grünhorn and other European assets; the businesses reported more than 12 tonnes dispensed or distributed and about 290,000 prescriptions fulfilled in the 12 months ended July 2026.

Why it matters: consolidation is increasingly about owning patient access, pharmacy/distribution and logistics — not only cultivation.

Regulation & Policy11 Sep 2026 · DK

Denmark reports strong 2025 growth as the programme moves into its permanent phase

The Danish Medicines Agency reported that redeemed prescriptions rose 64% in 2025 and the number of citizens redeeming them rose 54% versus 2024. Three suspected adverse-reaction reports were received for programme cannabis products in 2025.

Why it matters: Denmark combines measurable demand growth with a permanent legal framework from 1 January 2026, making it one of Europe’s cleaner markets for trend analysis.

Market & CompaniesH1 2026 data · PT

Portugal exported 66.3 tonnes in six months — while the operator base contracted

INFARMED-derived H1 figures show 66,305 kg exported, versus 79,883 kg in all of 2025. Germany received 37,442 kg, Spain 11,386 kg and Denmark 8,968 kg. H1 also recorded 5,413 domestic prescriptions, 43 licensed entities and 32 inspections.

Why it matters: Portugal is scaling as an export and processing hub while compliance pressure appears to be concentrating volume among fewer operators.

Regulation & Policy28 Aug 2026 · DE

Germany introduces a reimbursement priority for approved cannabis-containing finished medicines

A Bundestag notice describes a rule under which reimbursement of standardized cannabis extracts and medicines containing dronabinol or nabilone is generally preceded by at least a six-month treatment attempt with an approved cannabis-containing finished medicinal product.

Why it matters: reimbursement rules can shift demand between finished medicines, extracts and other formulations even without changing the basic legality of medical cannabis.

Wellford renews EU-GMP certification for another three years

Wellford says its Nova Scotia production facility renewed EU-GMP certification, preserving its ability to import and sell finished medical-cannabis products into the UK and Germany for a further three years.

Why it matters: certification continuity protects access to two major European markets and illustrates the value of compliant transatlantic supply chains.

Regulation & Policy11 Aug 2026 update · ES

Spain’s standardized-cannabis preparation register is operational

AEMPS now maintains the public register created under Royal Decree 903/2025 for standardized cannabis preparations that may be used in typified magistral formulas. Manufacturers must register preparations through the medicines-registration system.

Why it matters: Spain is not an open German-style flower market, but it now has a defined pharmaceutical route for standardized cannabis preparations.
AEMPS →
Regulation & Policy2 Sep 2026 · PL / EU

Polish pharmacists flag proposed changes to European Pharmacopoeia “Cannabis flos” monographs

The September issue of Aptekarz Polski highlights proposed changes to European Pharmacopoeia monographs including Cannabis flos, signalling another standards-development thread that quality and regulatory teams should follow.

Why it matters: pharmacopoeial changes can alter testing, specification and release expectations across European medicinal-cannabis supply chains.
