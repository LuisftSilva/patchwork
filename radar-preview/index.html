Evidence & methodology

How the public layer decides what can be presented as fact.

Primary-first publication rule

Regulators, public datasets, statutory registers, parliamentary records, procurement notices and company filings/releases are preferred. Specialized intelligence sources such as Cannamonitor are used to discover or contextualize information, but they do not replace primary evidence when a primary source exists.

OfficialRegulator, government, parliament, statutory or supranational source.
CompanyIssuer filing or company disclosure; clearly labelled as such.
SecondaryIndustry intelligence used for discovery/context and independently verified where material.
